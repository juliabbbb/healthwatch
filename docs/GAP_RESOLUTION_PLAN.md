## Read-only feasibility check

The leptospirosis CSV contains 30,605 nonblank source records and seven exact-duplicate candidates beyond first occurrences. Blank physical records are excluded; duplicate candidates remain because no patient identifier establishes that they are duplicate cases. Using the existing provisional ISO-Thursday/week-53 convention and the January 2019–August 2026 study window yields **29,476 provisional case-records**, mapped to all 18 regions. No invalid year/week or unmapped-region records were found in this check. Regions have 58–92 months containing records, so all exceed the 24-observed-month eligibility threshold even without assuming their absent months are zeros. This is a row-count coverage diagnostic, not a fitted forecast or proof of unique patients.

The current FWBD artifacts contain September 2026 records for ABD/Cholera/Typhoid, but the operational study cutoff remains August 2026. Hep A's last listed month is September 2025. First/last listed records are coverage diagnostics; they are not themselves evidence of a continuously covered reporting period. Do not turn Hep A's later months into zeros unless separate documentation establishes coverage there.
